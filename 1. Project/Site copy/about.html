About Thendo Events

Thendo Events is an event planning and catering business dedicated to creating memorable and enjoyable experiences for our clients.

We provide services for different occasions, including weddings, birthday celebrations, corporate events and other special gatherings.

Our Story

Thendo Events was created with the aim of helping people celebrate important moments without the stress of managing every detail themselves.

Our approach focuses on organisation, creativity, quality service and attention to the needs of every client.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide reliable event planning and catering services while creating experiences that clients and their guests will remember.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted event company known for quality service, creativity and successful events.
